How it works

🎵

Rewrites the rhythm

AI text is metronomic — same-length sentences, predictable beats. iHuman injects burstiness: some short. Some longer, winding thoughts that feel like a person thinking.

🧹

Kills AI clichés

"Delve into." "In today's fast-paced world." "Furthermore." The dead giveaways get replaced with plain language a human would actually write.

🔒

Private by design

No accounts, no servers, no tracking of your words. The rewriting engine is JavaScript running locally — close the tab and it's gone.
